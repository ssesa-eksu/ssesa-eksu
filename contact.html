FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about SSESA and the Department of Social Science Education.

How do I become a member of SSESA? ⌄
All registered students in the Department of Social Science Education at EKSU are automatically members of SSESA. You can become active by attending general meetings, paying dues, and participating in departmental activities.
What programmes are offered in the department? ⌄
The department offers B.Ed. in Social Science Education covering subjects including Economics Education, Government/Civic Education, Geography Education, and Social Studies Education at the undergraduate level. Postgraduate programmes are also available.
How can I participate in SSESA events? ⌄
You can participate by following our notice boards, attending general meetings, and following announcements from the SSESA executives. All departmental students are welcome at every event.
How do I contact the HOD of Social Science Education? ⌄
You can visit the Head of Department's office at the Department of Social Science Education, Faculty of Education, EKSU during working hours (Monday–Friday, 8am–4pm), or email the department at socialscienceedu@eksu.edu.ng.
Can I run for SSESA executive positions? ⌄
Yes! Any registered student in the department with good academic standing can contest for SSESA executive positions during the annual elections. Elections are held according to the SSESA constitution.
