About SSESA

Who We Are

The Social Science Education Students' Association (SSESA) is the official student body of the Department of Social Science Education at Ekiti State University (EKSU), Ado-Ekiti. We are a vibrant community of future educators and social science professionals committed to academic excellence and service.

Our Foundation

Mission, Vision & Values

🎯

Our Mission

To provide an enabling environment for Social Science Education students at EKSU to develop academically, professionally, and socially — equipping them to be impactful educators and responsible citizens in Nigerian society.

🔭

Our Vision

To be the foremost Social Science Education student association in Nigeria, producing world-class graduates who drive positive transformation in education, governance, economics, and community development.

⭐

Core Values

Excellence · Integrity · Unity · Service · Innovation · Academic Discipline · Respect for Diversity and the pursuit of knowledge in all social science disciplines.

Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence

The Department of Social Science Education at EKSU has a proud history of producing qualified teachers and social science practitioners who serve across Ekiti State and beyond.

1982 — Foundation

Ekiti State University established, with the Department of Social Science Education as one of its founding departments.

1995 — SSESA Founded

Social Science Education Students' Association formally constituted, giving students an organized platform for development.

2010 — Accreditation Milestone

Department achieved full NUC accreditation with commendations for quality teaching and research outputs.

2024 — Growing Forward

Continuing to expand postgraduate programmes and community outreach, with over 500 active students enrolled.
